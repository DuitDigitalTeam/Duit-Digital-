DUIT Digital Security

Building Trust Through Transparency

Security and trust are important foundations of the DUIT Digital ecosystem. We focus on responsible technology development, user awareness, and transparent communication.

User Protection Principles

🔐 Wallet Safety

Users should always protect their private keys and wallet information. DUIT Digital will never request private wallet credentials.

🛡️ Scam Awareness

The community is encouraged to verify official information and avoid suspicious links or impersonation attempts.

📢 Transparent Communication

Official updates, announcements, and ecosystem information will be shared through verified channels.

🤖 Responsible AI

DUIT AI is designed to provide information and guidance while maintaining human oversight for important decisions.

Important Security Rules

  • Never share your private key or seed phrase
  • Verify official DUIT Digital channels
  • Be careful with unknown links and messages
  • Understand Web3 technology before participating

Our Commitment

DUIT Digital aims to build a responsible ecosystem where technology, community, and transparency work together.

Security is not only about technology. It is also about education, awareness, and responsible participation.
